#### What is "Hash Rate"?

Hash rate is the estimated average number of hashes per second produced by miners on the network.

#### Why is "Hash Rate" important?

Hash rate reflects the total level of computational power devoted to mining blocks. A higher hash rate increases the difficulty of executing a 51% attack and therefore improves the network’s resistance to chain reorganization attempts. Changes in hash rate can indicate shifts in miner participation and changes in mining economics.

#### How is "Hash Rate" calculated?

Hash rate is estimated based on current network difficulty and average block time and reflects the approximate amount of computational power devoted to mining.
