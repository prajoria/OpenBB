#### What is "Ethereum new unique depositors"?

The number of new unique addresses that sent 32 ETH to the Ethereum staking deposit contract over the selected period. One validator does not necessarily correspond to one address - deposits can be sent from different addresses.

#### Why is "Ethereum new unique depositors" important?

The metric reflects the dynamics of new participants depositing funds for staking. An increase can indicate an expanding set of addresses participating in deposits.
