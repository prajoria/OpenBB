#### What is "Addresses with balance ≥ X (% of supply)"?

Addresses with balance ≥ X (% of supply) represent the count of unique addresses holding at least X percent of the total cryptocurrency supply. The default value is X = 0.001%, but users can select from multiple thresholds, including 0.00000001%, 0.0000001%, 0.000001%, 0.00001%, 0.0001%, 0.001%, 0.01%, and 0.1% to analyze different holder segments.

#### Why is "Addresses with balance ≥ X (% of supply)" important?

This indicator shows wealth concentration in the network, impacting market stability and manipulation risk. At the default 0.001% threshold, it counts addresses with significant positions that influence market dynamics. Lower thresholds (0.00001%-0.0001%) include more addresses with smaller stakes, revealing a broader holder base. Higher thresholds (0.01%-0.1%) focus on mega-whales. Fewer addresses at any threshold signals centralization. Healthy distribution shows many addresses at low thresholds and few at high ones, correlating with reduced volatility and organic price discovery.

#### How is "Addresses with balance ≥ X (% of supply)" calculated?

Addresses with balance ≥ X (% of supply) are calculated by counting unique addresses where balance ≥ (Current Supply × X/100). Users can adjust the X parameter in the indicator settings to analyze different concentration levels.
