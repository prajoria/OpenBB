#### What is "El Salvador Government balance in BTC"?

The amount of Bitcoin held by the government of El Salvador in publicly disclosed addresses, tracked for transparency and reporting purposes.

#### What is "El Salvador Government balance in USD"?

The value of the Bitcoin held by the government of El Salvador expressed in US dollars.

The data are published by the National Bitcoin Office (ONBTC) and reflect the current state of Bitcoin reserves.

#### Why are "El Salvador Government balance in BTC" and "El Salvador Government balance in USD" important?

The metric provides transparency regarding the Bitcoin reserves of a sovereign state. Changes in these indicators can reflect government operations with reserves - purchases, mining, or balance reorganization - and provide insight into the scale of governmental involvement in cryptoassets.

The BTC version shows the actual amount of the asset, while the USD version helps assess the economic size of these reserves given market movements.
