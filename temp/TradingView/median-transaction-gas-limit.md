#### What is "Median transaction gas limit"?

The median gas limit per transaction over the selected period.

#### Why is "Median transaction gas limit" important?

The median reflects the typical gas limit without being distorted by extremely high or low values. Unlike the mean, the median is less sensitive to outliers and provides a more accurate view of the gas-limit distribution set by users.

#### How is "Median transaction gas limit" calculated?

The median is determined by ordering all transaction gas limits over the period and selecting the central value of the distribution.
