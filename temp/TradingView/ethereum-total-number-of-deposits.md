#### What is "Ethereum total number of deposits"?

The cumulative number of transactions to the Ethereum staking deposit contract since its launch.

#### Why is "Ethereum total number of deposits" important?

The metric reflects the accumulated volume of deposit operations and the long-term dynamics of staking participation.
