#### What is "US spot crypto ETF flows"?

An estimate of the daily net change in the amount of cryptocurrency held by US spot ETFs, expressed in the asset’s native units.

#### What is "US spot crypto ETF flows in USD"?

An estimate of the daily net change in the amount of cryptocurrency held by US spot ETFs, expressed in US dollars.

#### Why are "US spot crypto ETF flows" and "US spot crypto ETF flows in USD" important?

The metric reflects the dynamics of capital inflows and outflows through regulated spot ETFs. Changes help assess the direction of fund movement into and out of cryptoasset-backed instruments.

The native-unit version reflects the actual net change in quantity held, while the USD version enables analysis of the economic scale of these flows.

#### How are "US spot crypto ETF flows" and "US spot crypto ETF flows in USD" calculated?

The metric is calculated by aggregating daily changes in holdings across all approved US spot crypto ETFs. A positive value indicates net inflows, a negative value indicates net outflows.

#### For BTC, the following funds are included:

  * BlackRock – IBIT
  * Grayscale – GBTC
  * Grayscale Mini Trust – BTC
  * Fidelity – FBTC
  * Ark/21 Shares – ARKB
  * Bitwise – BITB
  * VanEck – HODL
  * Valkyrie – BRRR
  * Invesco/Galaxy – BTCO
  * Franklin Templeton – EZBC
  * WisdomTree – BTCW



#### For ETH, the following funds are included:

  * BlackRock – ETHA
  * Grayscale – ETHE
  * Grayscale Mini Trust – ETH
  * Fidelity – FETH
  * 21 Shares – CETH
  * Bitwise – ETHW
  * VanEck – ETHV
  * Invesco/Galaxy – QETH
  * Franklin Templeton – EZET



Net flow is defined as the change in the aggregate fund balance versus the previous available value.
