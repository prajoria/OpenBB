#### What is "Ethereum new value staked"?

The amount of ETH transferred to the Ethereum staking deposit contract over the selected period, expressed in ETH.

#### What is "Ethereum new value staked in USD"?

The amount of ETH transferred to the staking deposit contract over the selected period, expressed in US dollars.

#### Why are "Ethereum new value staked" and "Ethereum new value staked in USD" important?

The metric reflects the volume of new funds allocated to staking over the period. An increase can indicate higher participation in securing the network. The USD version allows assessment of the economic scale of new deposits over time, accounting for changes in the ETH market price.
