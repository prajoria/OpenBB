#### What is "Ethereum new deposits"?

The number of transactions that deposited 32 ETH into the Ethereum staking deposit contract over the selected period.

#### Why are "Ethereum new deposits" important?

The metric reflects the pace of new deposits required to activate validators. An increase can indicate rising participation in proof-of-stake and an expanding validator set.

#### How are "Ethereum new deposits" calculated?

The metric is calculated by counting transactions that send 32 ETH to the Ethereum staking deposit contract over the selected period.
