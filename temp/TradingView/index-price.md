#### What is "Index price"?

Index price is a reference price of the underlying asset built from a basket of spot-market sources.

#### Why is it important?

  * It serves as a reference for derivatives valuation.
  * It is a baseline for metrics such as Premium and Basis.
  * It helps evaluate dislocations between derivatives and spot markets.



#### How is it calculated?

  * Index price is aggregated from multiple underlying spot price sources.
  * Source selection, weighting, and filtering are defined by the exchange methodology.
