#### What is "Mean transfer volume"?

The average value of successful transfer volume in the asset’s native units over the selected period.

#### What is "Mean transfer volume in USD"?

The average value of successful transfer volume expressed in USD.

#### Why are "Mean transfer volume" and "Mean transfer volume in USD" important?

The metric reflects the average size of asset movement on the network and helps assess the scale of economic activity. An increase in the mean can indicate a rising share of large transfers, while a decrease can indicate a predominance of smaller transactions.

The native-unit version shows the actual volume of asset transfers, while the USD version allows assessment of the economic scale of transfers, taking the market price into account.

#### How are "Mean transfer volume" and "Mean transfer volume in USD" calculated?

The mean is calculated as total successful transfer volume over the period / number of successful transfers.

The USD version is determined using the USD valuation of transfer volume over the same period.
