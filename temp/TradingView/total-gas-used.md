#### What is "Total gas used"?

The total amount of gas consumed by all transactions and smart-contract executions over the selected period.

#### Why is "Total gas used" important?

Total gas used reflects aggregate network activity and the volume of smart-contract interactions. An increase indicates higher network usage and rising demand for its resources.

The metric helps assess the scale of network activity and ecosystem development.

#### How is "Total gas used" calculated?

Total gas used is calculated as the sum of gas consumed by all transactions over the selected period.
