#### What is "Median UTXO value spent"?

The median number of coins in spent transaction outputs over the selected period, expressed in Bitcoin (BTC).

#### What is "Median UTXO value spent in USD"?

The median number of coins in spent transaction outputs over the selected period, expressed in US dollars.

#### Why are "Median UTXO value spent" and "Median UTXO value spent in USD" important?

The median reflects the typical size of UTXOs used in transactions without being distorted by extremely large or small values. Unlike the mean, the median is less sensitive to outliers and enables a more accurate assessment of the spend-size distribution.

The USD version additionally allows analysis of the economic significance of moved funds, accounting for market price changes.

#### How are "Median UTXO value spent" and "Median UTXO value spent in USD" calculated?

The median is determined by ordering all spent UTXO values over the period and selecting the central value of the distribution. For the USD version, values are converted to US dollars.
