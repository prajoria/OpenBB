#### What is "Mean gas used"?

The average amount of gas used per transaction, calculated as the ratio of total gas used to the number of transactions over the selected period.

#### Why is "Mean gas used" important?

Mean gas used reflects the overall complexity level of transactions on the network. Higher values typically indicate a higher share of complex smart-contract interactions, while lower values indicate a predominance of simple transfers.

The metric helps assess the structure of network activity and application usage patterns.

#### How is "Mean gas used" calculated?

Mean gas used = total gas used / number of transactions.
