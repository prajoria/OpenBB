#### What is "Transfer count"?

The total number of successful transfers of cryptocurrency during a specific period. It includes all movements of the asset on the blockchain.

#### Why is "Transfer count" important?

This metric helps analyze token usage dynamics and identify periods of increased load or market changes. An increase in the number of transfers typically indicates higher demand for the asset, an expanding user base, or increased network activity. A decrease may indicate declining interest, reduced liquidity, or a slowdown in economic activity within the blockchain.

#### How is "Transfer count" calculated?

Transfer count is calculated by summing all successful transfers on the blockchain during the measurement period.
