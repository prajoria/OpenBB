#### What is "Median transfer volume"?

The median value of successful transfer volume in the asset’s native units over the selected period.

#### What is "Median transfer volume in USD"?

The median value of successful transfer volume expressed in USD.

#### Why are "Median transfer volume" and "Median transfer volume in USD" important?

The median reflects the transfer size at the center of the distribution and is not sensitive to extremely large or extremely small values. This allows assessment of a more robust indicator of the transfer distribution and the typical scale of network activity.

The USD version provides comparability of the economic scale of transfers over time and across different assets.

#### How are "Median transfer volume" and "Median transfer volume in USD" calculated?

The median is determined by sorting all successful transfer volumes over the period and selecting the central value of the distribution.
