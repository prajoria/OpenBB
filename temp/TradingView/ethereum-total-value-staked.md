#### What is "Ethereum total value staked"?

The total amount of ETH currently held in the Ethereum staking deposit contract, expressed in ETH.

#### What is "Ethereum total value staked in USD"?

The total amount of ETH in the deposit contract expressed in US dollars.

#### Why are "Ethereum total value staked" and "Ethereum total value staked in USD" important?

The metric reflects the total amount of funds deposited to participate in proof-of-stake. An increase can indicate growth in the economic base securing consensus. The USD version enables assessment of the economic scale of staking over time.
