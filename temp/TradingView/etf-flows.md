#### What is "ETF flows"?

An estimate of the daily net change in the amount of cryptocurrency held by a spot ETF, expressed in the asset’s native units.

#### What is "ETF flows in USD"?

An estimate of the daily net change in the amount of cryptocurrency held by a spot ETF, expressed in USD.

#### Why are "ETF flows" and "ETF flows in USD" important?

The metric reflects the dynamics of capital inflows and outflows for a specific fund. A positive value indicates net inflows to the fund, while a negative value indicates net outflows. Changes help assess the direction of fund movement for that ETF and analyze its relative dynamics versus other spot funds.

The native-unit version reflects the actual net change in quantity held, while the USD version allows assessment of the economic scale of these flows.
