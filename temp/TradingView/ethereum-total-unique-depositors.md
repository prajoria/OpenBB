#### What is "Ethereum total unique depositors"?

The cumulative number of unique addresses that have sent 32 ETH to the Ethereum staking deposit contract since its launch. One validator may correspond to multiple addresses.

#### Why is "Ethereum total unique depositors" important?

The metric reflects the accumulated level of address participation in depositing funds for staking.
