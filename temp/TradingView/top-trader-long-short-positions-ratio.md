#### What is "Top trader long/short positions ratio"?

This metric compares long exposure to short exposure among accounts identified by the exchange as top traders for a selected derivatives instrument. Values above or below the baseline level indicate which side dominates.

#### Why is this metric important?

  * It shows directional imbalance in top-trader exposure.
  * It helps evaluate whether long or short positioning conviction is stronger.
  * It can highlight changes in risk appetite among top traders
