# Long / Short Ratio Accounts

**Source:** <https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000762399-long-short-ratio-accounts/>  
**Indicator ID:** 43000762399  
**Slug:** `long-short-ratio-accounts`

[← back to index](./README.md)

---

A trader sentiment metric that shows the proportion of accounts holding long versus short positions for a specific trading pair or symbol. This metric reflects the number of accounts with a net position — not the trade volume or position size. If an account holds a net long position, it is counted as one long account, regardless of how large the position is; the same applies to accounts with a net short position.

The ratio is calculated as Long/Short Ratio = Number of Long Accounts / Number of Short Accounts.

On the chart, the data is available as the "Long / Short Ratio Accounts" indicator in the "Financials" tab.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/43578103235/original/VAXiy589-j9vNly15Q2e_bn4j6Y_lPY6pQ.png?1756728557)

"Long / Short Ratio Accounts" is available for crypto derivatives of the following exchanges: BYBIT, BINANCE.
