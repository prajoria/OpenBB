#### What is "Mean transaction gas limit"?

The average gas limit per transaction over the selected period. Gas limit is the maximum amount of gas the sender sets for executing a transaction.

#### Why is "Mean transaction gas limit" important?

The metric reflects the average gas limit selected by transaction senders. Changes can be associated with changes in the nature of operations, for example, a higher share of transactions interacting with smart contracts.

#### How is "Mean transaction gas limit" calculated?

Mean transaction gas limit is calculated as the sum of all transaction gas limits divided by the number of transactions over the selected period.
