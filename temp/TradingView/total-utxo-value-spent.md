#### What is "Total UTXO value spent"?

The total number of coins in spent transaction outputs over the selected period, expressed in BTC.

#### What is "Total UTXO value spent in USD"?

The total number of coins in spent transaction outputs over the selected period, expressed in USD.

#### Why are "Total UTXO value spent" and "Total UTXO value spent in USD" important?

The metric reflects the aggregate amount of funds used as transaction inputs over the period. An increase may indicate a higher volume of fund movement on the network.

The USD version enables assessment of this amount in a comparable monetary denomination and analysis of dynamics over time.

#### How are "Total UTXO value spent" and "Total UTXO value spent in USD" calculated?

Total UTXO value spent is calculated as the sum of all values of spent UTXOs over the selected period. For the USD version, values are converted to US dollars and summed.
