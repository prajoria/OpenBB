#### What is "Top trader long/short accounts ratio"?

This metric compares the share of long and short accounts among accounts identified by the exchange as top traders for a selected derivatives instrument. Values above or below the baseline level indicate which side is dominant.

#### Why is this metric important?

  * It shows directional imbalance in top-trader account positioning.
  * It helps identify whether long or short accounts dominate.
  * It is useful for tracking sentiment shifts over time.
